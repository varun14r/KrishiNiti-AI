# Crop Selection and Seasonal Planning

- Crop choice should consider season, rainfall, temperature, soil type, irrigation availability, local market demand, and the farmer's available resources.
- For kharif conditions in much of India, commonly grown crops include rice, maize, cotton, soybean, groundnut, millets, pulses and vegetables depending on the region.
- For rabi conditions, common crops include wheat, chickpea, mustard, lentil and selected vegetables depending on climate and irrigation.
- In Karnataka, crop suitability varies substantially by district. A recommendation should therefore request the farmer's district/taluk, soil type, irrigation availability and intended season before giving a specific recommendation.
