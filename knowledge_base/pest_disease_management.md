# Integrated Pest Management

- Integrated Pest Management combines monitoring, prevention, cultural practices, mechanical methods, biological control and, when necessary, carefully selected pesticides.
- Farmers should regularly inspect leaves, stems, flowers and fruits for insects, eggs, larvae, disease symptoms and abnormal growth.
- Preventive practices include crop rotation, field sanitation, resistant or tolerant varieties where available, balanced fertilization and removal of severely affected plant material when recommended.
- A pest or disease should not be diagnosed from one symptom alone. Ask for crop age, affected plant part, symptoms, location and photographs when available.
