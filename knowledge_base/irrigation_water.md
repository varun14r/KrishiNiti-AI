# Water and Irrigation Management

- Efficient irrigation aims to provide the crop with adequate water while reducing runoff, evaporation and waterlogging.
- Drip irrigation can be useful for many row crops, horticultural crops and vegetables because water can be delivered near the root zone.
- Sprinkler irrigation can be useful for several crops and soils where suitable.
- Irrigation timing should consider crop growth stage, soil moisture, weather and rainfall forecasts. Avoid unnecessary irrigation immediately before substantial rainfall when practical.
