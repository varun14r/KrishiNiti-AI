# Soil and Nutrient Management

- Soil testing is an important first step before making fertilizer recommendations. A soil test can provide information such as pH and available nutrients.
- Organic matter can be improved through compost, well-decomposed farmyard manure, crop residues and suitable green-manure crops.
- Fertilizer application should be based on crop requirement and soil-test results rather than applying the same dose to every field.
- Very acidic or alkaline soils can restrict nutrient availability. Soil amendments should be selected according to soil-test results and local recommendations.
