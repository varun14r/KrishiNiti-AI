# Common Crop Problems and Safe Guidance

- Yellowing leaves can have several causes, including nutrient deficiency, water stress, root problems, pests or disease. A diagnosis requires additional field information.
- Wilting can result from insufficient water, root damage, vascular disease, waterlogging or other stresses. Check soil moisture and inspect roots and stems before recommending treatment.
- Leaf spots, powdery growth, curling, holes and discoloration may be associated with different pests or diseases. Avoid recommending a specific pesticide solely from a vague symptom.
- When pesticide use is considered, the farmer should follow the product label, approved crop/pest use, dosage, safety interval and protective-equipment requirements, and seek local agricultural guidance when diagnosis is uncertain.
