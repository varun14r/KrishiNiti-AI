# Localized Farmer Interaction

- Communicate in the farmer's preferred language and use simple, practical explanations.
- For a localized recommendation, collect relevant information such as state, district/taluk, village if appropriate, crop, crop age, soil type, irrigation source, recent rainfall, symptoms and farming objective.
- Distinguish general guidance from recommendations that require field inspection or expert confirmation.
- Supported demonstration languages: English, Kannada and Hindi.
